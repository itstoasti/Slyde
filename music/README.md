# 🎵 Slyde Audio Engine & Music Library

This folder stores the background audio tracks automatically muxed into your 60 FPS recipe videos for **TikTok**, **Instagram Reels**, and **YouTube Shorts**.

## 🚀 How It Works
When Slyde renders a video from your recipe slides:
1. It selects an audio track from this folder (based on your chosen vibe or random rotation).
2. It loops, normalizes, and applies smooth 0.3s fade-in and 0.6s fade-out using FFmpeg.
3. The video is uploaded to TikTok and Instagram with full high-fidelity stereo sound!
4. TikTok and Instagram recognize this as **"Original Sound — SnapRecipes"**, preserving full algorithmic reach with zero copyright strikes.

## 📂 Adding Your Own Tracks
You can drop any of your favorite audio files directly into this directory:
- Supported formats: `.mp3`, `.wav`, `.m4a`, `.aac`
- Recommended length: 10 seconds to 60 seconds (tracks will automatically loop or trim to match your video length).
- Naming suggestions:
  - Prefix with vibe keywords like `lofi-`, `acoustic-`, or `upbeat-` so they match your vibe presets in the Bulk Scheduler.

- `acoustic-coffee-vibes.wav`: Bright, warm acoustic guitar arpeggios.
